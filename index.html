    Getting there

    Fly into Goa. The state has two airports, and either works.

    GOX Mopa (Manohar International) About 45 min to Morjim · closest
    GOI Dabolim About 1 hr or more to Morjim

    Flying from abroad

    There are no flights from the US straight to Goa. Most people connect through Mumbai, Delhi or Bengaluru, or through the Gulf; a few Gulf cities, including Dubai, have direct flights to Goa.

    From the airport

    Take a prepaid taxi from the counter in arrivals, where the fare is fixed before you leave. GoaMiles, the state's taxi app, is the other reliable option. Uber and Ola run in Goa but can be hard to get at the airports.

    Visas

    Most people without an Indian passport or OCI card need an Indian e-Visa. Apply only on the official site, indianvisaonline.gov.in; look-alike sites charge extra.

    Time zone

    Goa runs on India Standard Time, UTC+5:30: nine and a half hours ahead of New York in late March.
