    Getting there

    Fly into Goa. The state has two airports, and either works.

    GOX Mopa (Manohar International) About 45 min to Morjim · closest
    GOI Dabolim About 1 hr or more to Morjim

    Flying from abroad

    There are no flights from the US straight to Goa. Most people connect through Mumbai, Delhi or Bengaluru, or through the Gulf; a few Gulf cities, including Dubai, have direct flights to Goa.

    Visas

    Most people without an Indian passport or OCI card need an Indian e-Visa. Apply only on the official site, indianvisaonline.gov.in; look-alike sites charge extra.

    Worth a detour

    • Ashwem & MandremQuiet beaches just up the coast from Morjim, 10–15 min away.
    • Chapora FortClifftop fort ruins with views over the river mouth, about 20 min away.
    • ArambolA laid-back beach town with a sunset drum circle, about 30 min north.
    • Anjuna & VagatorBeach cafes and shacks; Anjuna's flea market runs on Wednesdays.
    • Fontainhas, PanjimGoa's old Latin quarter of Portuguese-era houses, about an hour away.
    • Old GoaUNESCO-listed churches, including the Basilica of Bom Jesus.
    • Further afieldKerala's backwaters and Kochi are a short flight away. Hampi's ruins and Gokarna's beaches are long drives, so plan an overnight.

    Staying safe

    • In the seaCurrents can be strong. Swim only where lifeguards have set out flags, and not after drinks or after dark.
    • On the roadScooters are everywhere, but you need an International Driving Permit and a helmet. Take a taxi at night.
    • Going outStick to busy, well-lit places, keep an eye on your drink, and book cabs through your hotel or a prepaid counter rather than hailing one.
    • Travelling onThe US State Department advises women not to travel alone in India. If you're adding Delhi, Agra or Rajasthan, go with friends or a guided tour rather than solo.

    Time zone

    Goa runs on India Standard Time, UTC+5:30: nine and a half hours ahead of New York in late March.
