    Getting there

    Fly into Goa. The state has two airports, and either works.

    GOX Mopa (Manohar International) About 45 min to Morjim · closest
    GOI Dabolim About 1 hr or more to Morjim
    Flying from abroad

    There are no flights from the US straight to Goa. Most people connect through Mumbai, Delhi or Bengaluru, or through the Gulf; a few Gulf cities, including Dubai, have direct flights to Goa.

    Visas

    Most people without an Indian passport or OCI card need an Indian e-Visa from the official site, indianvisaonline.gov.in. Apply four to six weeks before you fly, around mid-February. The official minimum is four days, but give yourself time to fix any problems. Your passport needs to be valid for at least six months after you arrive.

    Places to see in Goa
    • Ashwem & MandremQuiet beaches just up the coast from Morjim, 10–15 min away.
    • Chapora FortClifftop fort ruins with views over the river mouth, about 20 min away.
    • ArambolA laid-back beach town with a sunset drum circle, about 30 min north.
    • Anjuna & VagatorBeach cafes and shacks; Anjuna's flea market runs on Wednesdays.
    • Fontainhas, PanjimGoa's old Latin quarter of Portuguese-era houses, about an hour away.
    • Old GoaUNESCO-listed churches, including the Basilica of Bom Jesus.
    Travelling on

    By late March most of India is hot: Delhi, Agra, Rajasthan and Hampi are often in the mid-30s °C (mid-90s °F). If you're adding a trip, the hills are cooler and lovely in spring.

    • Munnar, KeralaRolling tea estates high in the Western Ghats. Fly to Kochi, then about 4 hours by road.
    • Ooty, Tamil NaduAn old hill town in the Nilgiris, with a heritage toy train up the mountain. Fly to Coimbatore, then about 3 hours by road.
    • Darjeeling & SikkimTea gardens, Himalayan views and rhododendrons in bloom. Fly to Bagdogra, then 3–5 hours by road.
    • Dharamshala & McLeod GanjHimalayan foothills with Tibetan monasteries, cafes and mountain walks. Fly to Kangra via Delhi.

    The US State Department advises women not to travel alone in India, so go with friends or a guided tour rather than solo.

    Time zone

    Goa runs on India Standard Time, UTC+5:30: nine and a half hours ahead of New York in late March.
